## v1.8.6

- Fixes [#219](https://github.com/Han-1413141/dsh-cost-meter/issues/219): conversation cost details now declares an explicit `role="dialog"`, allowing skin styles that target `[role=dialog]` to restore the dialog background and inherited backgrounds of its controls. The fix applies to both the header and composer entry points.

## 中文

- 修复 [#219](https://github.com/Han-1413141/dsh-cost-meter/issues/219)：单会话费用明细弹窗补上显式 `role="dialog"`，使皮肤的 `[role=dialog]` 规则生效，恢复弹窗及内部控件的背景。标题栏和输入框下方的入口均已修复。
